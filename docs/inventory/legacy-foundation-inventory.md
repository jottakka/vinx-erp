- Discovery used `rg` only inside that explicit checkout. Counts were: 102 unique tables from 106 declarations, 0 views, 60 unique functions from 74 declarations, 176 logical policies from 204 declarations, 6 buckets, 233 unique routed paths from 234 route declarations, 33 service files, 8 Edge Functions and 12 direct Supabase/auth integration files.

- A future schema-only snapshot may use `pg_dump --schema-only --no-owner --no-privileges --schema=foundation --schema=audit`; it must not include data or DDL execution.
